Corrections to the filed report

Issued because this report is a public record. Both items are corrected here and in the full report below; the filed PDF is otherwise unchanged.

  1. Section 3 — Programme delivered

    Filed text reads Storytellers introduced as ‘Storyteller for the Convening.’

    The plural is incorrect. One Storyteller for the Convening was introduced and performed: Chezik Tsunoda, founder of No More Under. More than one was programmed; one appeared. Every public-facing record has been corrected to the singular.

  2. Section 12 — Attachments, by link

    Several URLs did not render in the filed PDF: the Converge Media address is truncated, a stray character follows the archive line, and the King County, Primo’s Bellevue and TikTok entries carry no address at all.

    The complete, working list is set out under The record below and is permanently maintained here.

Still outstanding

Pack-the-Box delivery. Approximately $250 in school supplies and gifts is held pending designation of a receiving school. ToyBox will confirm the recipient and delivery date to the City in writing. This is the one contract deliverable not yet closed.
