What you built

Twenty vendor and community tables. Two proclamations — one from the City of Bellevue, one from King County Executive Girmay Zahilay naming August Black Business Month. A commissioned spoken-word piece from Melissa Margain, Chezik Tsunoda telling her story, Jermaine Murray pitching Fathers Against Human Trafficking, and a sunset toast.

It was free to every guest, and it started and ended on time.
 

You’re in the record

goldenhourunboxed.com is now the record: the program as it ran, thirty captioned photographs, and the partners who paid for it. Several of you’re in the photographs.

Primo’s Bellevue catered the evening and donated the service labor. They posted their own reel from the night — worth a look.

Converge Media also cut a five-minute piece from the night — the people who were there, on camera, saying in their own words why the room mattered. It’s on Facebook, and the comments underneath kept going after the video did. That’s where the conversation actually lives. Go read it, and add to it if you were there.
 

What people said, unprompted

Nobody was asked for a testimonial. These are what people posted publicly on their own inside seventy-two hours, or said to Converge Media’s camera on the night.

“I’ve attended many networking events where the energy felt transactional… the environment Katoya cultivated exuded warmth and undeniable vibrancy. It felt like a big happy family coming together to support and learn from one another. Reciprocity was at the heart of the event.”

— Brad Blackburn III, MPA, Identity Unboxed

“Thank you to everyone who stopped by our booth, took home a piece of our curated collection, and brought such amazing energy. A huge thank you for hosting such a vibrant event and bringing this incredible community together.”
