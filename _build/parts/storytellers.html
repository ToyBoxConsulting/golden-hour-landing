Storytellers for the Convening

The voices we'll gather around.

A small circle of founders, makers, and builders we have invited to carry their stories into the evening.

Each Storyteller for the Convening wears a corsage at the event. Look for the corsage and you have found someone with a story worth a moment of your time — about how they started, what they have built, and what they are still becoming. Stories happen on a walk through the garden, in conversation in the crowd, or briefly on stage as the sun softens.

Five storytellers. Three minutes each, on what it actually took to build the thing they built — including the parts that don't photograph well. Their names are announced from the stage on the night.
