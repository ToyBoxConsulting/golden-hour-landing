Public servants who saw the vision early and lent their offices to lift Black Business Month on the Eastside.

Proclamation Received · August 4, 2026
"Bellevue welcomed me with wide open arms, and made room for me at table after table. I want every Black business owner building in this city to feel that — because every entrepreneur deserves that kind of welcome."

— Katoya R. Palmer, accepting the City of Bellevue's proclamation declaring August 2026 as Black Business Month, delivered by Deputy Mayor Dave Hamilton on behalf of Mayor Mo Malakoutian and the Bellevue City Council.

Proclamation Signed · King County
"Black Business Month takes on particular meaning on the Eastside, where a new generation of Black founders, makers, and small business owners is reshaping the cultural and economic fabric of our communities."

— King County Executive Girmay Zahilay, proclaiming August 2026 as Black Business Month and recognizing Black Entrepreneurship on the Eastside. Angela Phan, East County Relations Manager in the King County Executive Office, attended in person.
