Nonprofit Partner Charitable giving & community programming
Eastside for All

Eastside For All is our nonprofit partner for this event. A DBA of Alliance for a Just Society, a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, they are an East King County organization led by and for Black and brown community members, organizing for a safe, welcoming, and just Eastside. They receive, receipt, and safeguard every charitable gift made to Golden Hour Unboxed. eastsideforall.org
