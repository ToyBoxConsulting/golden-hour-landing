Sponsor Golden Hour Unboxed 2027.

Year one proved the night works. Year two is about doing it without asking anyone to absorb the cost.

What the City covered. A 10,000 dollar contract from the City of Bellevue paid for the venue, the permits, the licensing and the logistics. It did that job well, and it is the reason the evening was free to every guest.

What it did not cover. Almost everything guests actually experienced. The photography and the video, the host, the commissioned spoken word piece, the event-day coordination and the catering labor were donated or discounted — more than sixty hours of given time, alongside nine volunteers who worked the night from setup through teardown. Golden Hour Unboxed happened because those people said yes. We are still saying thank you.

What year two needs. This year was the bare minimum and we made the best of it. Next year is not a different event — it is the same evening with a few more milestones: vendors and community tables at this scale or larger depending on our venue partnership, the team paid for their work, and the founders prize funded before it is announced rather than after.

The demand is already here. Vendors have asked to come back, and guests have asked for a winter market — a second night indoors, with the same room and the same care — and there is an appetite for an arts-led edition. Golden Hour Unboxed was built as one evening a year. With the right partners it could be a season.

Sponsorships and gifts are received through Eastside For All, our nonprofit partner and a DBA of Alliance for a Just Society, a 501(c)(3), so your support is a charitable contribution.
