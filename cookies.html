What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device. We also use similar technologies (local storage, session storage, web beacons / pixels, tracking scripts). For simplicity we call them all "cookies." Some are deleted when you close the browser ("session cookies"); others stay for a set period ("persistent cookies").

How to manage cookies

  • On this site: Use the "Open cookie settings" button above. Most pages also have a "Cookie settings" link in the footer.
  • In your browser: All major browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies. See your browser's help pages.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC): On toyboxconsulting.net, a GPC signal switches off visitor identification and advertising automatically. katoyapalmer.com and goldenhourunboxed.com run no advertising or visitor-identification tools.
  • Google Analytics: install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity: leave analytics off, or turn it off, in cookie settings.
  • RB2B: leave "Visitor identification" off, or turn it off, in cookie settings on our tools pages.
